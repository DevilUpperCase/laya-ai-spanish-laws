# Document research, notebooks and official-publication monitoring

Status: owner confirmed design and implementation on 2026-10-12. This document records Q1–Q9 answered on 2026-10-12. It does not claim implementation or national coverage.

## Confirmed product behavior

1. View original documents inside the app. Uploaded PDFs have an authenticated original preview; DOCX has extracted text and original download. Official results have a reader for the exact section version used by retrieval, plus the original publication PDF where the publisher supplies it. Label original publication, consolidated version, publication date, version date and unknown effectiveness separately. Missing PDFs produce an explicit availability notice, never a fabricated URL or replacement document.
2. Keep the prohibition on generative LLMs. Improve query understanding through local semantic retrieval and an optional **Refinar consulta** interview: legal issue, desired outcome, territory and relevant date. Unknown answers are allowed. Show editable interpreted input and filters before searching. Direct search remains available. Preserve the original question and never silently infer a legal fact.
3. Every result explains its actual retrieval path: exact identifier, lexical retrieval and/or semantic retrieval, rank contributions, applicable explicit filters, and any attached-document passage used as context. Show literal evidence and navigable source anchors. Similarity and LAYA classification are signals, not legal opinions or calibrated probabilities of applicability. LAYA remains mandatory in shadow mode with the existing outage fallback.
4. Search can upload new PDF/DOCX files or select multiple existing active workspace files. Explicit modes are **Buscar publicaciones oficiales** and **Buscar en documentos adjuntos**. Select relevant stored passages for official-publication retrieval; do not concatenate entire documents into a question. Private search returns literal passages, document/page links and explanation signals. Queued/failed files cannot silently count as searched. Attachment permissions and deletion/trash state are rechecked before returning results.
5. **Documentos** becomes a shared workspace library: folders/case notebooks, searchable file listing, rename/move, original preview, upload progress, handwritten notes, document/page/selection links and saved official references. Existing admin/lawyer roles can edit; reader can read. Save explicit notebook content only; transient queries and LangGraph state are not persisted. Notes are plain text and safe links, never rendered arbitrary HTML. Concurrent edits require a revision check rather than silently overwriting.
6. **Papelera** is recoverable indefinitely until an authorized user empties it or permanently deletes an item. Moving a document there hides it and its linked notes from ordinary library/search/attachment views; the trash view can display originals and linked notes and restore them. General notebook notes remain. Permanent deletion immediately revokes every access path, purges originals/text/vectors/document-linked notes and saved copied excerpts within 24 hours, and expires encrypted backup copies within 30 days. Restore rehearsals reapply permanent deletion records before access. Existing deletion tombstones are never resurrected or reinterpreted as recoverable trash.
7. Expand public-corpus scope to all publicly accessible items and attachments in approved official bulletin catalogues, including administrative notices, tenders, appointments, grants and judicial notices published there. This includes distinct bulletin catalogues such as BOE and BORME where required by the source inventory. Keep **Normativa** and **Todas las publicaciones** separate; default search is Normativa. Individual notices are labelled publications and never treated as general rules. Standalone agency websites and court-judgment databases are outside this expansion. The national scope retains every required jurisdiction and current plus officially available history.
8. Provide an on-demand processing checker and a daily reconciliation/check script. Report independently: required jurisdiction/publisher inventory, expected issues/items/attachments, archived originals and hashes, parse/index progress, missing/failed records, available-history ranges, last reconciliation and freshness. A complete finite snapshot is different from nationwide completion. Unsupported sources, unknown expected counts, missing history or unaudited coverage block a national-complete result. Daily checks report failure/staleness honestly and preserve previous success evidence; computer downtime cannot count as a successful daily run.

## Architecture and constraints

FastAPI supplies typed routes; LangChain Core carries public and private retrieval interfaces; LangGraph coordinates interpreted queries, attachment resolution, retrieval and explanations; local BGE-M3 supplies 1024-dimensional embeddings; real local LAYA supplies shadow classification. PostgreSQL/pgvector, authentication and storage remain self-hosted. No external inference/tracing, private query logs or persisted private search graph state. Product UI remains Spanish; code and documentation remain English.

Use separate entities for original bulletin items and normative instruments. Preserve relationships between an original publication and its consolidated instrument without duplicating or conflating their dates. Non-normative items have no inferred legal-effectiveness interval. Archive trusted publisher URLs/bytes/hash/format and keep source-specific discovery/reconciliation evidence. No general URL proxy or user-supplied source fetch.

Separate trash time from permanent-deletion time. RLS and API predicates enforce tenant membership and active/trash visibility. Page/chunk/notes foreign keys contain the tenant identifier. Authenticated original preview uses the same fixed browser gateway and tenant headers as download; it must not expose temporary storage URLs that bypass revocation. Render the PDF from an authorized blob with cleanup on workspace change, logout, deletion or closing the viewer; browser bytes already delivered cannot be remotely erased.

Private passage vectors are document derivatives, stored under the same tenant, trash and deletion lifecycle. Deterministic passage splitting has literal offsets and OCR uncertainty. Bounded candidate and inference budgets produce visible truncation/degradation warnings. Territory filters include the selected jurisdiction's relevant ancestors and descendants without treating every national source as proof of local coverage.

## Quality and LAYA training decision

Measure baseline versus improved recall/ranking on professionally reviewed positive, negative, temporal and multilingual cases. Include queries where the relevant law uses different words, attached context is misleading, and the relevant document is absent. Existing 200 drafts are not ground truth. Preserve the original frozen holdout; use a new calibration dataset for query-planner changes.

Evaluate LAYA separately from retrieval. Training becomes justified only if reviewed candidate labels show a reproducible classifier deficiency and held-out comparison demonstrates improvement. Training cannot repair missing corpus or unindexed passages. No private document enters a training export by default. Any later trained checkpoint needs an audited training interface, immutable model hash, licensing/resource review, held-out evidence and a rollback; production ranking still requires its separate approval gate. This design authorizes measurement and training-readiness tooling, not invented training data or an unevaluated checkpoint.

## Observable acceptance

- A real BOE result opens its exact stored version and a publisher-supplied original PDF when available; a missing original is labelled. User PDF preview and DOCX download work with live workspace isolation.
- Every result shows actual retrieval signals and literal source/context evidence; no word-overlap-only explanation claims conceptual relevance.
- Both attachment modes work with at least two real synthetic uploaded files, and refuse foreign-tenant, trashed, permanently deleted or unprocessed attachments.
- Folder/file listing, notes and saved references survive refresh. Readers cannot write. Revision conflicts are visible.
- Trash exclusion, original/notes visibility in trash, restore, empty-trash and irreversible purge are tested, including worker races and restoration of a backup made before permanent deletion.
- Daily/on-demand reports distinguish processing-complete from national-complete, reject unknown expected coverage, flag stale/missing runs and verify archived-file integrity.
- Real-model and browser checks supplement offline tests; all unexecuted professional, source-completeness, performance or training approvals remain blocked.
